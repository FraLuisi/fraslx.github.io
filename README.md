## Selected Projects

### Volatility & Volatility-of-Volatility Research

My main research interest is the interaction between volatility and volatility-of-volatility indices and their potential applications in forecasting and quantitative market analysis.

The research follows a progressive cross-market framework:

**US Market → European Market → Italian Market**  
**VIX–VVIX → VSTOXX–VVSTOXX → VVFTSEMIB**

---

### 1. VIX–VVIX Predictability | US Market

**MSc Thesis**

My MSc thesis investigates whether the VVIX contains incremental predictive information for forecasting the VIX beyond the information already embedded in the historical dynamics of the VIX.

The analysis compares HAR and HAR-VVIX models across multiple forecast horizons and examines whether the predictive contribution of the VVIX changes across market regimes and periods of financial stress.

**Methods:** HAR models, time-series forecasting, Granger causality, rolling analysis, HAC inference, out-of-sample evaluation, Clark–West and Diebold–Mariano tests  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Statsmodels, Scikit-learn

[View Project and Code](https://github.com/FraLuisi/VIX-VVIX-Analysis)

---

### 2. VSTOXX–VVSTOXX Predictability | European Market

**Next Research Project**

The second stage extends the VIX–VVIX framework to the European equity market.

The objective is to investigate whether the predictive relationships identified between the VIX and VVIX are also present between the VSTOXX and VVSTOXX, providing a cross-market assessment of volatility-of-volatility predictability.

**Planned Methods:** HAR models, time-series forecasting, rolling analysis, out-of-sample evaluation and cross-market comparison  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Statsmodels

**Status:** Planned

---

### 3. VVFTSEMIB | Italian Market

**Volatility-of-Volatility Index Construction**

The final stage aims to extend the volatility-of-volatility framework to the Italian equity market by developing a volatility-of-volatility measure associated with the FTSE MIB volatility market.

The project will investigate whether the empirical patterns identified in the US and European markets can be reproduced in the Italian market and whether the constructed measure contains useful information about future volatility dynamics.

**Working Name:** VVFTSEMIB  
**Research Focus:** Volatility index construction, options, volatility-of-volatility and predictive analysis

**Status:** Planned


### 4. International Extension | International Markets

**International Extension**

The fourth stage extends the volatility and volatility-of-volatility framework to additional international equity markets.

The objective is to investigate whether the relationships identified in the US, European and Italian markets generalize across different financial markets, and whether volatility-of-volatility provides systematic predictive information across countries with different market structures and volatility dynamics.

Where established volatility-of-volatility indices are not available, the project will explore the feasibility of constructing market-specific volatility-of-volatility measures using a methodology consistent with the previous stages of the research.

**Research Focus:** Cross-market predictability, volatility-of-volatility, international equity markets, index construction and comparative analysis  
**Methods:** Time-series forecasting, HAR models, out-of-sample evaluation, cross-market comparison and robustness analysis  
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Statsmodels

**Status:** Planned
